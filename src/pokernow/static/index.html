Three ways to use this

Everything table-wide works without logging in. Your own un-shown hole cards are the only thing that needs more — here's how to add them.

1 · Watch & analyze any game default — no login

Paste a game link above. You get every hand, the boards, all cards shown at showdown, the ledger, player stats, and luck-vs-the-table — for everyone at the table. The only thing missing: hole cards that were never shown (including your own).

2 · After the session: upload your log full personal insights — easiest

Because you download it while logged in, PokerNow's own log export contains your hole cards for every hand.

  1. On your PokerNow game page, open the log (⋮ menu or the LOG panel) and click Download full log to save the CSV.
  2. Drop that CSV on this app's home screen.

That's it — the hole-card insights (cards dealt, flop hits, hand groups) unlock. No cookie, no setup.

3 · Live tracking with your own cards advanced — login cookie

To see your hole cards while the app fetches or live-tracks, it must ask PokerNow as you. PokerNow identifies you by a browser cookie named npt:

  1. Open PokerNow in the browser where you're seated at (or logged into) the game.
  2. Open developer tools — ⌘⌥I on Mac, F12 on Windows — and go to Application (Chrome/Edge) or Storage (Firefox/Safari) → Cookies → the PokerNow site.
  3. Find the cookie named npt, copy its value (a long token), and paste it here:

🔒 Treat this like a password — it is your PokerNow identity. It is kept only in this app's memory on this machine, never written to disk, and only ever sent back to PokerNow. It's forgotten when the server restarts.
